How to plan your desk cable management

  • Start with the power strip. Most cable mess comes from one strip and its plugs. Get it off the floor into an under-desk tray, or hide it in a box like the CableBox, and most of the clutter goes with it.
  • Work in zones. Use a tray under the desk, a spine or sleeve for the run to the floor, and clips on the desktop. One product rarely solves all three.
  • Leave slack for standing desks. If your desk moves, raise it to its highest setting before tying anything down so cables don't pull tight. Our standing desks for small spaces guide lists height ranges for each desk.
  • Measure your desk underside first. Check desk thickness and where the frame and crossbars are before choosing between a screw-in tray and a clamp-on one.
  • Use reusable ties, not zip ties. You will add or replace a cable eventually; hook-and-loop ties make that a one-minute job instead of a cutting job.
