How to choose a desk lamp for your home office

  • Decide between a lamp and a light bar. If you mostly look at a monitor, a light bar lights the desk without taking space or reflecting off the screen. If you read, write or sketch on paper, a lamp with a wide or movable head is more flexible.
  • Look for adjustable color temperature. Cooler light (around 5000K and up) feels crisper for focused work during the day; warmer light (around 2700–3000K) is easier on the eyes in the evening.
  • Avoid glare on your screen. Place a lamp to the side, not behind the monitor, and angle the head down. Light bars are designed to avoid screen glare.
  • Check how it mounts. Weighted bases need surface space; clamps need a desk edge that's thin enough and not blocked by a frame or drawer. If your desk is small, see our standing desks for small spaces guide for layout ideas.
  • A monitor arm changes the setup. If your screen is on an arm, check that a light bar will clip on securely and won't block a webcam. Our monitor arms guide covers popular arms.
