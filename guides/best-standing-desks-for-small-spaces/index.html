How we chose

We haven't lab-tested these desks ourselves. Instead, we compared manufacturer specifications, read through owner reviews for recurring complaints (wobble, noise, motor failures) and narrowed the list to desks 48 inches wide or smaller. When we test products in person, we will say so clearly.
