How to choose a standing desk mat

  • Measure the floor in front of your desk. The mat should cover where your feet actually go, usually 20–24 in deep and at least as wide as your shoulders plus some room to shift. For a compact desk, see our standing desks for small spaces guide.
  • Flat or contoured? Flat mats give even cushion and let a chair roll close. Contoured mats like the Topo and TerraMat nudge you to change position, which many people find helps more than extra softness.
  • Thicker isn't always better. Around 3/4 in is the common sweet spot for flat mats. Very soft foam can make you sway and work your ankles harder.
  • Polyurethane lasts longer than basic foam. PVC and similar foams are cheaper but tend to compress over time. Polyurethane mats cost more and usually keep their bounce longer.
  • Think about your chair. If you switch to sitting often, pick a thinner mat with beveled edges, or one light enough to slide aside.
